Privacy Policy

FeedbackOS collects only the account, workspace, survey, response, billing, and operations data needed to run the local feedback platform.

Data We Store

Account profiles, workspace memberships, survey schemas, response answers, metadata, contacts, files, integrations, jobs, billing records, audit logs, and error logs may be stored in the configured data store.

Workspace Isolation

Workspace data is scoped by workspace membership and role checks. Public survey respondents can only access published public survey surfaces.

Privacy Controls

Workspace admins can configure anonymous mode, IP/user-agent collection, retention days, exports, and deletion or anonymization requests.

Prototype Provider Notes

In this local build, email, AI, billing, and provider integrations are simulated unless production provider credentials are configured.
